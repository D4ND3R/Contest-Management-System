# Clarifications, announcements and messages

How organizers and contestants talk during the contest, and what each side
sees. Everything arrives **live**: the contestant gets a notice on whatever
page is open, without reloading, and administrators see new questions at
once.

## What the contestant sees

One page, **Clarifications**, with a table (newest first):

| *About* column | What it is |
|----------------|------------|
| Everyone · *Announcement* | A general announcement of the organizers. |
| You · *Private message* | A message for that contestant (or team) only. |
| A problem or General · *Your question* | Their question and, once it comes, the answer. |
| ... · *Your question, answered for everyone* | Their question, whose answer was published for all. |
| ... · *Clarification for everyone* | Someone else's question answered publicly. |

The form to ask is on top (about a problem or *General*). Each novelty
brings an on-screen notice, raises the red number of the
**Clarifications** menu and, if the contestant turned it on, plays a sound.

## What organizers do

### Answering questions

**Clarifications** (main menu) or **Questions** (top right, with the
number of pending ones) opens the inbox of every contest. Filters choose
the contest, the problem, whether to include answered or ignored ones, and
only the ones you took.

Each question shows who asked, about which problem, when, and its state
(*pending*, *answered*, *public*, *ignored*). To answer:

1. **take it** (optional, recommended with several organizers: the others
   see you have it; **give back** releases it);
2. a **quick answer** button (*Yes*, *No*, *No comment*, *Read the
   statement*, ...) and/or a text in **Answer**;
3. **Show the question and the answer to everyone in the contest** when the
   answer helps everybody (a clarification of the statement, for example):
   every contestant sees it as a *Clarification for everyone*; otherwise
   only the asker sees it;
4. **Send answer**.

**ignore** removes a question that deserves no answer (spam, a duplicate);
**back to pending** brings it back.

### Announcements to everyone

In the contest: **Announcements** (second menu row). Write a **Subject**
and a **Text** and **Publish to everyone**. Every contestant gets the
notice at once and it stays on their Clarifications page and on the
Problems page. **delete** withdraws it.

### Private messages

On the same page, **Private messages**: **To** a user (or a team code, for
the whole team), subject and text. Only that contestant sees it.

## Tips

- If a question reveals a mistake in the statement, fix the statement
  (contestants get a notice and the text reloads by itself) and also answer
  publicly explaining the change.
- Keep answers short and neutral; quick answers avoid giving hints by
  accident.
- Each contestant's questions per minute are limited (**Settings → All
  other settings → Access**).
